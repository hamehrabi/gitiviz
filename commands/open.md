---
description: Open the generated gitiviz HTML change book
allowed-tools: ["Bash(open:*)"]
---

Run: `open .gitiviz/dist/index.html`

If `.gitiviz/dist/index.html` does not exist, do not invent output — tell the
user to generate the book first with `/gitiviz:branch`, `/gitiviz:commit <sha>`,
or `/gitiviz:compare <base> <head>`.
